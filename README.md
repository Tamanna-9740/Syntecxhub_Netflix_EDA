# 🎬 Project : Netflix Content Analysis

## 📌 Objective

Explore trends in Netflix content (Movies vs TV Shows).

## 🔍 Key Analysis

* Content distribution (Movie vs TV Show)
* Year-wise content growth
* Top genres analysis
* Runtime distribution
* Top 10 release years

## 🧠 Insights

* Movies dominate Netflix content compared to TV Shows
* Rapid content growth observed after 2015
* Drama and International genres are most frequent
* Most content duration falls within standard movie runtime
* Recent years show aggressive expansion in content production
